LISS-4 ClearNet

Multi-Modal SAR-Optical Fusion for Automated Cloud Removal in High-Resolution Earth Observation

Team: Beyond the Clouds  |  Challenge: Cloud Removal in LISS-4 Images

The Monsoon Optical Blind Spot

  • Cloud Cover Obstruction: Persistent monsoon cloud cover blocks optical cameras (LISS-4) during critical agricultural cycles.
  • Indian Farm Resolution: Average farm holdings are extremely small (< 1 ha). Global 10m sensors suffer from spectral mixing; LISS-4's 5.8m resolution is essential.
  • Information Loss: Standard interpolations fail to preserve spectral fidelity under clouds, ruining vegetation classification.
ISRO Core Requirement Continuous, cloud-free 5.8m optical imagery is critical for PMFBY crop insurance verification and crop classification.

Multi-Sensor Data Fusion

  • SAR Wave Penetration: Sentinel-1 C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) waves penetrate clouds completely, bouncing off the crop structures.
  • Cross-Modal Mapping: ClearNet maps geometric backscatter textures from the SAR stream into the optical reflection space.
  • Simulated LISS-4 Output: Generates synthetic Green, Red, and Near-Infrared (NIR) bands matching the target sensor grid.

ClearNet Architecture

  • Dual-Branch Encoder: Separates feature learning for 3-band cloudy optical and 2-band SAR inputs.
  • Multi-Head Cross-Attention: Fuses structural radar keys into the optical stream at a downsampled resolution to prevent VRAM overflow.
  • Deep Residual Decoder: Synthesizes cloud-free pixel values using a loop of 16 ResNet blocks.
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( (Q Kᵀ) / √d_k ) V

NDVI Vegetation Preservation Loss

  • Spectral Distortion: Standard generators distort spectral ratios, ruining vegetation index calculations.
  • Joint Loss Formulation: Enforces pixel-wise matching alongside index preservation:
L_total = L_MSE + λ L_NDVI
  • NDVI Formula: Ensures the ratio between Near-Infrared (NIR) and Red channels is preserved:
NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Data Preprocessing Pipeline

  • UTM Grid Warping: Automatically resamples 10m Sentinel-1 SAR onto the 5.8m LISS-4 coordinate grid using bilinear warping.
  • Amplitude-to-dB Calibration: Converts raw integer amplitude to logarithmic decibels to match the expected training scale:
    dB = 20 * log10(amplitude) - 58.0
  • Standardized Scaling: Restricts optical to [0.0, 1.0] and scales SAR db to match the training distribution of [-0.003, 0.0].

Integration with Bhoonidhi & Bhuvan

  • Bhoonidhi Ingestion: Automatically triggers when new LISS-4 scenes have > 10% cloud cover.
  • Level-3 (L3) Product: Outputs georeferenced GeoTIFFs, making cloud-cleared imagery available in the Bhoonidhi catalog.
  • Downstream Applications: Integrates directly into Bhuvan’s agricultural monitors and national crop insurance claims (PMFBY).
